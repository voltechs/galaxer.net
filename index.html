About the Game
Real-Time Space Conquest

Galaxer is a real-time multiplayer strategy game set in the depths of space. Claim planets, build your fleet, and wage interstellar war against rival commanders.

Every planet you own generates ships over time. Deploy your growing fleet to overwhelm neutral worlds or strike enemy territory. The galaxy is procedurally generated each round, so no two games play the same.

Simple rules, deep strategy. One match can shift from calm expansion to all-out galactic warfare in seconds.

How to Play
Four Steps to Galactic Domination
Master these basics and the galaxy is yours.
01 🌏

Claim Planets

Start with your home world. Send ships to nearby neutral planets to expand your territory.

02 ⚙️

Build Fleets

Each planet generates ships over time. Larger planets produce faster. Grow your empire to outpace rivals.

03 🚀

Deploy Ships

Select a planet and send your fleet toward any target. Ships fly in real time across the map.

04 ⚔️

Conquer

Overwhelm enemy planets with superior numbers. Eliminate all opponents to win the galaxy.

The game

A galaxy you win by moving faster than everyone else

Every planet you hold quietly builds ships. Fling those fleets at neutral worlds to grow, or at a rival's stronghold to break them. There's no base-building menu to master and no resources to micro — just planets, fleets, and the reflexes to commit at the right second. One match swings from quiet expansion to an all-out neon brawl in seconds.

How it plays

Four moves to the last planet standing

The whole game runs on one loop. Learn it in a match, spend a lifetime timing it.

Objectives
1

Claim planets

Start on your home world and send colonizers to the nearest neutral planets. Territory is production.

2

Grow the fleet

Held planets build fighters on their own — bigger worlds build faster. Upgrade factories and defense to snowball.

3

Deploy in real time

Fling fighters across the map with a right-click. They fly live — every launch is a commitment with no take-backs.

4

Conquer

Overwhelm a defended world, flip it to your color, and repeat until no rival commander is left.

Built for

Fast reads, clean fights, real stakes

Multiplayer, up to 16

Authoritative server with client-side prediction over reliable UDP. Host a lobby or join one and fight up to fifteen others at once.

Instant to read

Color is ownership, size is strength, motion is intent. The whole board state lands at a glance so the decisions are yours, not the interface's.

Neon renderer

A purpose-built HDR pipeline with real bloom draws every planet, fleet, and laser — the Geometry-Wars glow is the game telling you what's alive.

Live 30 Hz simulation

Ships move under a real-time physics sim, not turns. Timing, angles, and reading the room beat brute numbers.

Campaign + Skirmish

Learn the loop solo against the AI, then take it online. Every skirmish galaxy is procedurally seeded, so no two rounds repeat.

Native macOS

A real macOS app — universal for Apple Silicon and Intel, built on SDL3. No browser, no launcher, no account. Download, right-click, play.
